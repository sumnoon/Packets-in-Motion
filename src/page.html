        Engineering missions

        Design a system that survives real growth

        1. Read the brief. Each mission names the traffic, the failures to survive and a budget.
        2. Shape your system. Choose app servers, a load balancer, a cache and later a second region. The diagram updates as you go.
        3. Test all conditions. Every condition must serve its traffic within budget. If a check fails, the result tells you what to change.

        Pass a mission to unlock the next one. Your design carries over, so each mission builds on the last. Missions are separate from lesson stars, and nothing is timed.
